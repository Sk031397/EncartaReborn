# Encarta Reborn — Product Principles

## What this is
Encarta Reborn is a calm, offline-first, algorithm-free digital encyclopedia for
exploring real places on Earth. It resurrects the dead CD-ROM encyclopedia
(Encarta, 1993–2009) — the last great model of bounded, browsable, serendipitous
learning — and reimagines it for 2026 as the deliberate opposite of the
doomscrolling attention economy.

Tagline: **"The encyclopedia that doesn't scroll back."**

## The problem we solve
People no longer learn about the world on their own terms. Algorithmic feeds
shape opinions through engagement-optimized outrage, infinite scroll, and
pre-chewed narratives. Slow, self-directed, curiosity-led discovery has nearly
disappeared. Encarta Reborn brings it back.

## Who it is for
Students, curious adults, travelers, parents who want their kids learning without
a feed, and anyone doing a digital detox who still wants to learn.

## The anti-feed manifesto (non-negotiable design laws)
These rules override convenience. If a feature violates one, it does not ship.

1. **No infinite scroll.** Content is bounded and paginated like articles and
   cards, never an endless stream.
2. **No algorithmic recommendations.** "Related trails" are curiosity links
   derived from the knowledge graph (shared topics, geography, categories),
   never from engagement signals or personalization that optimizes for time-on-app.
3. **No engagement metrics shown to the user.** No likes, view counts, trending,
   streaks-as-pressure, or comment sections.
4. **No tracking, no accounts required.** The app works without sign-in and does
   not phone home. Any personal data (expedition log, notes) stays on-device.
5. **Every factual claim must be sourced.** All facts come from public, open
   datasets and each card carries a visible citation. No unsourced opinions.
6. **Offline-first.** The experience must work with no internet connection, in the
   spirit of a CD-ROM. The network is an enhancement, never a requirement.
7. **Calm by default.** Motion is gentle, sound is optional and off by default,
   nothing autoplay-grabs attention, nothing creates urgency.

## Approved data sources (public / open only)
- **Wikipedia / Wikidata** — article summaries, structured facts.
- **OpenStreetMap / Natural Earth** — geography, boundaries, place data.
- **NASA / public-domain imagery** — satellite and space imagery.
- **UNESCO World Heritage** — cultural and natural heritage sites.
- **Public-domain / Creative Commons media** — images and audio with clear licenses.

Every datum rendered to the user must trace back to one of these, with the
license and source recorded. If a source cannot be cited, the content is not shown.

## Core experience (the loop)
1. **Boot / "insert disc"** nostalgic start screen.
2. **Explore** a globe or stylized map; spin or pick a place.
3. **Article** view: sourced summary, public-domain image, key facts — every claim cited.
4. **Related trails** rail: 3–5 curiosity links to keep wandering.
5. **Expedition log**: the places you visited and your own notes — your discovery,
   not an algorithm's.

## Tone of voice
Warm, curious, unhurried, factual. Never clickbait. Never urgent. We invite
wandering, we do not demand attention.
